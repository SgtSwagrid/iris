Other

com.alecdorrington.iris.openai.OpenAiModel.Other
final case class Other extends OpenAiModel

A model Iris does not list, such as one newer than Iris or a proxy's own. Nothing is known of it but its name, so it is sent as OpenAi's newest models are. Made by of, so that a listed model is always its listed case.
