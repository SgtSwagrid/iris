final class Budget[F[_]]

A limit on what requests may cost together, in US dollars, for a session or any other span a host chooses: every prompt service it limits spends from it, whatever its model or provider. A request is refused once as much as the limit has been spent, and is charged what its response cost when the response comes. Its cost is known only then, so requests already sent when the limit is reached are charged in full, and may take the spending past it, by as many requests as are in flight at once: a stream running then among them, which goes on to its end. A stream stopped before its end reports no usage, so is charged nothing, though the provider may charge for what it wrote. What is spent never resets, so a budget for each day, say, is a new budget each day.

A budget needs prices: a response with no PromptResponse.cost, as from a model Iris does not list whose configuration gives no PromptConfig.rates, fails its request with PromptError.Unpriced. By then the provider has charged for it, and the reply is lost, as is every later one from that model, so give such a model rates before limiting it.

Value parameters
