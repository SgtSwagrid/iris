final case class Other extends LlmModel

A model Iris does not list, such as one newer than Iris or a proxy's own. Nothing is known of it but its name, so it is sent as its provider's newest models are: without Anthropic's sampling parameters, for one. Made by LlmModel.of, so that a listed model is always its listed case.

def parse(name: String): Option[LlmModel]

Parses a model from its name in its provider's API, which must be one Iris lists, in any case, or else from that name after its provider's and a colon, as anthropic:claude-sonnet-5-5, which may name any model.
